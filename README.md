***The Amazing Spider-Man***


> "With great power, comes great responsibility."

*Description*\
The Amazing Spider-Man is a 2012 fantasy movie directed by Marc Webb about a teenager named Peter Parker, played by Andrew Garfield, who gains spider-like abilities after getting bitten by an unknowingly radioactive spider. This earned him the nickname, "Spider-Man", who goes along within the borders of New York City to save citizens who are in danger.\
